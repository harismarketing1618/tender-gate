# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Primary: Pakistani civil contractors, MEP engineering firms, and technical estimation teams registered with the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC Categories C-A, C-B, C-1, C-2, C-3, C-4, C-5, C-6).
- Secondary: Public procuring officers (Federal PPRA, provincial PPRAs, NHA, WAPDA, IDAP, MES, CDA), material suppliers (steel, cement, bitumen), and construction consultants.

## Product Purpose
TENDER GATE is Pakistan's premier autonomous construction procurement and tender intelligence platform. It eliminates fragmented procurement discovery by continuously indexing, structuring, and auditing public sector tenders across 24+ federal and provincial government e-procurement portals.

## Positioning
The only multi-agent AI procurement network specifically engineered for Pakistan's construction sector that pairs autonomous portal crawling with automated PEC licensing compliance verification, 2% Call Deposit Receipt (CDR) bond calculation, and instant WhatsApp intelligence alerts.

## Operating Context
- Commercial estimation desks preparing bid dossiers under strict closing deadlines.
- Rapid morning triage (08:00 to 10:00 PKT) before bank cutoff times for Call Deposit Receipt (CDR) issuance.
- Mobile field engineers and contractor executives reviewing real-time WhatsApp tender digests on job sites.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **8 Autonomous AI Crawlers:** Category-specific bots indexing highways (Sheer-Khan), commercial high-rises (Memar), MEP/HVAC (Barq), dams/water (Aab), defense/MES (Muhafiz), municipal C5/C6 works (Balad), bridges/flyovers (Pul-Saz), and architectural consulting (Naqsha).
- **PEC License Eligibility Matcher:** Automatic verification of contractor category limits (C-A unlimited to C-6 up to PKR 25M) and engineering specialization codes (CE-01, BC-01, EE-04, ME-01, etc.).
- **Automated Financial Audit:** Computes 2% Earnest Money (CDR), 10% Performance Guarantees, and provincial stamp duties for any tender value.
- **Deep Search & Filtering:** Multi-token full-text search across reference IDs, procuring agencies, provinces, cities, and required BOQ scopes.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison:** Multi-tender comparison matrix for evaluating up to 3 competitive tenders simultaneously.
- **Tender Alert Digests:** Configurable WhatsApp and Email notification channels tailored by province and PEC category.

## Brand Commitments
- **Brand Name:** TENDER GATE
- **Aesthetic:** High-precision engineering and architectural intelligence, deep slate/navy palette with warm stone/beige accents.
- **Voice & Tone:** Authoritative, transparent, compliant, and deeply attuned to Pakistani public procurement procedures.

## Evidence on Hand
- Live dataset with verified tender notices from Federal PPRA, NHA, WAPDA, IDAP, SPPRA, KPPRA, MES, and CDA.
- Verified Pakistan construction material indices (Steel Grade 60, Cement OPC, Bitumen 60/70, Diesel).
- Production-ready responsive React 19 + Vite web application.

## Product Principles
1. **Zero Information Asymmetry:** Every public tender across Pakistan should be accessible, structured, and auditable in one central directory.
2. **PEC & PPRA Compliance by Default:** All tenders must feature clear PEC category eligibility, required engineer points, and 2% CDR calculations.
3. **Speed to Bid Decision:** Enable estimation teams to qualify or disqualify bidding opportunities in under 30 seconds.
4. **Frictionless Delivery:** Provide instant sharing and alerts through Pakistan's dominant communication channel (WhatsApp).

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast status indicators for closing urgency and PEC licensing badges.
- Fully responsive interface optimized for both wide multi-monitor estimating workstations and mobile on-site review.
